# Rubric — Behavioral Interview

Score each 0–5. Uses STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result).

| Dimension | 0–1 | 2–3 | 4–5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Structure** | Rambling | Loose STAR | Crisp STAR, easy to follow |
| **Ownership** | "We/the team" only | Some personal role | Clear *I* did X, owned outcome |
| **Impact** | No result | Qualitative result | Quantified result + what it changed |
| **Reflection** | No learning | Generic lesson | Specific change made afterward |
| **Signal fit** | Misses the ask | Partially addresses | Directly evidences the trait probed |

## Traits to probe (rotate)
ownership · dealing with conflict · handling failure · influencing without authority ·
prioritization under ambiguity · mentoring/raising the bar · disagreeing with a decision.

## Interviewer notes
Push past rehearsed answers: "what specifically did *you* do," "what would you do
differently," "how did you measure the impact." Log the trait probed and whether the
story actually evidenced it.
